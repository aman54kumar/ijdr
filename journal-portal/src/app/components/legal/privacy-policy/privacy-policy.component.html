Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

Last updated: 9 April 2026

1. Information We Collect

The Indian Journal of Development Research (IJDR) collects information to provide better services to our users. We collect information in the following ways:

Personal Information

  • Account Information: When you create an account or submit a manuscript, we collect your name, email address, institutional affiliation, and contact details.
  • Research Information: Manuscript submissions, peer review data, and academic credentials.
  • Communication: Records of your communications with our editorial team.
  • Website contact form: If you use the contact form on ijdrpub.in, we collect the name, email address, and message you submit. These submissions are stored in our journal website database (Google Firebase / Firestore) so editorial staff can read and respond. They are not displayed publicly. Access to this data is restricted to authorised administrators.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, including pages visited and time spent.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser type, device information, and cookies.
  • Analytics: Website performance and user interaction data through Google Analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Publishing Services: To process manuscript submissions, conduct peer reviews, and publish accepted articles.
  • Communication: To send editorial decisions, publication notifications, and journal updates.
  • Website Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our website functionality.
  • Research Integrity: To maintain the quality and integrity of our publication process.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and institutional requirements.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Peer Reviewers: Manuscript details (without revealing author identity) for the review process.
  • Published Articles: Author names and affiliations in published research articles.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or legal process.
  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate our website and publishing platform, including Google (hosting, analytics, and cloud database for the website and contact form) where their terms and privacy policies apply.
  • AI-assisted cataloguing: To build the article list of an issue, our editors may send the PDF of an issue that has already been published on this site to Google's Gemini API. Under Google's free-tier terms, such inputs may be used by Google to improve its models. Unpublished manuscripts are never sent through this process, and AI-extracted details are reviewed by an editor before they are shown publicly.

3A. AI-Powered Reader Features

Some article pages offer optional AI features, which an administrator can switch off at any time:

  • Summaries and Hindi translation: the text of a published article (its pages from the published issue PDF, or its abstract) is sent to Google's Gemini API once to produce a summary or translation. The result is stored on our servers and shown to all readers, labelled as AI-generated. Translations may contain errors; the English original is authoritative.
  • Ask this paper: the question you type and the pages of the article you are reading are sent to the Gemini API to produce an answer. We do not store your question, the answer or any chat history. To limit abuse we keep a daily usage counter linked to a one-way hash of your network address, not the address itself.
  • Meaning-based search and related articles: article titles, abstracts and keywords (and, for search, the phrase you type) are converted to numeric embeddings by the Gemini API.
  • Google: under Google's free-tier terms, data sent to the Gemini API may be used by Google to improve its models. Do not type personal or confidential information into the question box. We use Google reCAPTCHA through Firebase App Check to protect these features from automated misuse.

AI can make mistakes; please verify important points in the article.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS)
  • Secure server infrastructure
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication measures
  • Staff training on data protection practices

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal information.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal and contractual obligations).
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal information.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service.
